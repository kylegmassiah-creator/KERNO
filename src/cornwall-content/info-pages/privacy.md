Cornwall Orienteering Club takes your privacy seriously and only uses personal
information for running the club and its events.

- **Membership** is administered by British Orienteering, and member data is held
  under British Orienteering's privacy policy.
- **Event entries** are handled through entry systems such as SI Entries, which
  hold your data under their own privacy policies. We use entry details only to
  run the event and publish results.
- **Results and photographs** from events may be published on this site and on
  our social media. If you'd prefer a result or photo not to appear, please
  contact us.
- We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

To ask about the information we hold, or to request a correction or removal,
please get in touch via the [contacts page](/contacts).

*This is a short summary; a fuller policy can be provided by the committee on
request.*
