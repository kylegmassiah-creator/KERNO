---
title: Age classes
summary: How orienteering age classes work, based on the age you reach during the calendar year.
section: participation
order: 2
---

As well as by colour, courses can be grouped by age class. Your age class is set
by the age you reach on 31 December of the competition year, so if you turn 40 in
December, your class is M/W40 from the January of that year. (M/W means Men or
Women.) Elite (E) classes are sometimes offered in M/W18, 20 and 21.

| Your age | Class |
| -------- | ----- |
| 10 and under | M/W10 |
| 12 and under | M/W12 |
| 14 and under | M/W14 |
| 16 and under | M/W16 |
| 18 and under | M/W18 |
| 20 and under | M/W20 |
| Any age | M/W21 |
| 35 and over | M/W35 |
| 40 and over | M/W40 |
| 45+, 50+, 55+ ... in 5-year bands | M/W45 to M/W85+ |

For newcomers, age classes don't matter, just pick a colour course that suits
you. Classes mainly come into play at larger events and in the rankings.
