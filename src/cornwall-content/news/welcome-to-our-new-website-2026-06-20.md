We're delighted to launch the new Cornwall Orienteering Club website. You'll
find our upcoming events, results, mapped areas and information about how to get
started - all in one place.

Cornwall Orienteering Club (KERNO) has been promoting the sport across Cornwall
since 1982. Whether you're brand new to orienteering or a seasoned competitor,
there's a course for you. We look forward to seeing you in the woods, on the
coast and across the moors.
