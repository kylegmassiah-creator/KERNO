These resources are mainly for members planning or organising KERNO events.
British Orienteering publishes detailed guidance which is well worth using, even
for experienced planners.

**Planning**

- Course planning guide, and per-colour advice (White through to Black) and
  sprint courses, are available from British Orienteering.
- Guide to IOF control descriptions.

**Organising**

- Guidance on running events with SportIdent, start procedures, road signs,
  map stock, helper lists and risk assessments (forest and non-forest).

If you'd like copies of KERNO's templates and the latest British Orienteering
guidance, contact the committee via the [contacts page](/contacts).
