You don't need an event to go orienteering. KERNO helps maintain permanent
orienteering courses (POCs) with fixed posts you can visit any day:

- **Tehidy Country Park, Camborne** - woodland country park in west Cornwall. [Course details and map](https://www.goorienteering.org.uk/course/tehidy_country_park)
- **St Clement Woods, Idless, Truro** - Forestry England woodland just north of Truro. [Course details and map](https://www.goorienteering.org.uk/course/idless_woods_fc)

Download the map from the links above, head out when it suits you, and find the
controls at your own pace. They're a great way to practise between events.

For permanent courses across the rest of the country, see British Orienteering's
[GoOrienteering](https://www.goorienteering.org.uk/permanent-orienteering-courses).
