Cornwall Orienteering Club is committed to providing a safe and welcoming
environment for everyone who takes part, and follows British Orienteering's
safeguarding policies and procedures.

Our Welfare Officer is **Pauline Olds**. If you have a welfare or safeguarding
concern, please speak to the Welfare Officer or any committee member, or contact
us via the [contacts page](/contacts).

For the club's full safeguarding policy and British Orienteering's guidance, see
the [contacts page](/contacts) to reach the Welfare Officer, or British
Orienteering's [safeguarding pages](https://www.britishorienteering.org.uk/).
