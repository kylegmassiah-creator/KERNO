title: Juniors and families
summary: Orienteering is one of the most family-friendly outdoor sports. Here's how children and families can take part.

---

Orienteering suits families brilliantly: everyone navigates their own course at
their own pace, then meets back at the finish.

- **String courses** for very young children are sometimes available at events, where they follow a line of string between fun controls, often with their own simple map.
- **White and Yellow courses** are designed for children to do alone or alongside an adult.
- At **Street-O and urban events**, juniors under 16 can take part accompanied by an adult. Some urban courses are designed to stay within a park or only cross quiet roads.
- Children who have not orienteered before should start on White or Yellow with an adult to help with the map.

Families are welcome at every KERNO event, and there is usually a pairs or family
entry option. If you're coming with children for the first time, just say so at
registration and a helper will get you started.
