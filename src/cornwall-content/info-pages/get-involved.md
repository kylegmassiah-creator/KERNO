Every KERNO event happens because volunteers make it happen, and there's a role
for everyone, whether you've been orienteering for years or just a few weeks.

- **On the day:** helping at registration, download or the start, or collecting
  controls afterwards. No experience needed, and it's a great way to learn.
- **Planning courses:** designing the courses for an event. British Orienteering
  provides guidance for new planners, and experienced members will mentor you.
- **Organising:** coordinating an event, from permissions and parking to
  registration and results.
- **Mapping:** surveying and updating our orienteering maps.
- **Behind the scenes:** photography, social media, the newsletter, or helping
  with results.

If you'd like to help, even occasionally, please [get in touch](/contacts), come
along to an event and have a chat, or speak to any committee member.
