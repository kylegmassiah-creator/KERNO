title: Top tips for beginners
summary: Five simple skills that make a big difference when you start orienteering.
section: participation

---

A few basic habits help you progress quickly:

1. **Fold your map** so you can clearly see the part you're in.
2. **Orientate your map** so features in front of you on the ground are in front of you on the map. A compass helps: line the north lines up with the needle.
3. **Thumb your map** - mark your position with your thumb and move it on as you reach each feature.
4. **Check the control code** matches your description sheet before you dib, so you know it's the right one.
5. **Have fun.** Orienteering should always be enjoyable, slowing down often helps you read the map better and actually speeds you up.
