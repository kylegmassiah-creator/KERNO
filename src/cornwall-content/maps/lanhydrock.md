The National Trust estate at Lanhydrock, near Bodmin, gives a mix of formal
parkland, estate woodland and tracks. The KERNO map is continually evolving as
the estate changes, and it is a popular venue for both newcomers and
experienced orienteers.

Possession of a map does not imply any right of access; permission is arranged
with the landowner for each event.
