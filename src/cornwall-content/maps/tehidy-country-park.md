Tehidy Country Park is the largest area of woodland in west Cornwall, with a
network of paths and rides that make it ideal for orienteering. It is home to
one of KERNO's permanent orienteering courses, so you can pick up a map and
navigate the controls any day of the year.

Maps and full details for the permanent course are on British Orienteering's
GoOrienteering site (linked above).
