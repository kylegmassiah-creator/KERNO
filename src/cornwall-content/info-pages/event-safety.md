Orienteering is a safe sport, and a few simple habits keep it that way.

- **Always report to the finish or download**, even if you don't complete your
  course, so officials know everyone is safely back. This is the golden rule.
- **Full leg cover** is normally required on terrain courses to protect against
  undergrowth. A whistle is sometimes compulsory and will be flagged in the
  event details.
- **Clothing and footwear:** dress for the weather and the terrain. Trail shoes
  with grip are best off-road; a lightweight waterproof is wise in changeable
  Cornish weather.

**Ticks** are common in areas where we orienteer. It's worth knowing how to
check for and safely remove them after an event; British Orienteering and the
NHS both publish simple guidance.
